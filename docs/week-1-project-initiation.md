## Project Charter

### Project

**AI Club Member and Event Management Platform**

### Purpose and business need

AI clubs need a reliable way to keep member information current, communicate opportunities, and organize events. When these activities are spread across separate tools or manual records, it can be difficult to coordinate participation and understand engagement. This project will define and deliver a centralized platform to support those core workflows.

### Vision

Provide AI clubs with an accessible, easy-to-use platform for member engagement, event management, and data-informed growth.

### Objectives

- Maintain a centralized, permission-controlled member directory and membership records.
- Let organizers publish events and manage registration and attendance in one place.
- Give members a clear way to discover events and receive club updates.
- Provide organizers with basic participation and engagement summaries.
- Deliver an MVP that club representatives can evaluate against agreed acceptance criteria.

### Proposed success measures

The project team and sponsor should confirm baselines and targets before implementation. Suggested MVP measures are:

- All approved test member accounts can view and update the member information permitted to them.
- Organizers can create, publish, update, and cancel an event, and review registrations.
- Members can find and register for an event; organizers can record attendance.
- Organizers can view an accurate summary of membership and event participation.
- Pilot users complete the agreed core workflows and provide feedback for a prioritized follow-up backlog.

### High-level deliverables

- A web-based MVP for member and event workflows.
- Role and access rules for members and club organizers.
- Basic reporting for membership and event participation.
- User guidance and a pilot feedback process.

### Governance and authorization

The project sponsor, project manager, delivery team, funding, schedule, and formal approval authority are **to be assigned**. Until these are confirmed, this charter is a planning baseline rather than an authorization to incur costs or handle real member data.

### Initial assumptions and constraints

- The initial product is intended for one AI club or a small pilot; multi-club support requires confirmation.
- A web experience is sufficient for the MVP; native mobile applications are not assumed.
- Any collection or use of member data must be approved and follow applicable institutional privacy, security, and accessibility policies.
- Budget, hosting environment, integrations, and launch date have not been supplied and remain open decisions.

## Scope Statement

### In scope for the initial MVP

- Member registration or invitation and a centrally managed member directory.
- Member profile viewing and updates within approved role permissions.
- Role-based access for members and authorized organizers.
- Event creation, publishing, editing, cancellation, registration, and attendee lists.
- Attendance recording by organizers.
- Basic announcements or event-related updates.
- Basic views or reports for membership and event participation.
- Usability, accessibility, privacy, and security checks appropriate to the approved pilot.
- Pilot deployment, user guidance, and collection of feedback.

### Out of scope unless separately approved

- Native iOS or Android applications.
- Payment processing, ticket sales, or financial accounting.
- Advanced analytics, predictive models, or automated member scoring.
- Integrations with external calendars, learning-management systems, or identity providers beyond what is confirmed for the pilot.
- Support for multiple independent clubs, chapters, or tenants.
- Importing historical member data without an approved source, migration plan, and privacy review.
- Long-term operations or service-level commitments beyond the agreed pilot.

### Key requirements and acceptance conditions

- Members can access only the information and actions permitted by their assigned role.
- An organizer can manage an event from publication through attendance recording.
- Members can discover and register for available events and receive clear registration status.
- Participation summaries reconcile with the underlying event and attendance records.
- The pilot has documented user guidance and an agreed path for reporting issues.
- Sponsor and designated club representatives approve the MVP against agreed test scenarios before wider release.

### Dependencies and open decisions

- Confirm sponsor, product decision-maker, project manager, and pilot club.
- Confirm schedule, budget, hosting, supported browsers/devices, and deployment ownership.
- Decide how users authenticate and how membership is verified.
- Approve the minimum member data collected, retention period, and access policy.
- Confirm whether announcements and integrations are required for the pilot.
- Define baseline and target values for success measures.

### Change control

Requests that add features, users, integrations, or operational commitments beyond this baseline should be recorded, assessed for impact on schedule, cost, risk, and privacy, and approved by the designated sponsor or product decision-maker before inclusion.

## SWOT Analysis

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- |
| Clear focus on the connected needs of member engagement and event coordination. | Sponsor, team, budget, schedule, and decision authority are not yet identified. |
| A centralized platform could reduce fragmented records and duplicated organizer effort. | Requirements, user volumes, existing processes, and technical environment are not yet validated. |
| An MVP and pilot provide a way to learn from real users before expanding scope. | Member-data handling creates privacy, access-control, and maintenance responsibilities. |
| Initial scope can be kept focused on common club workflows. | Adoption may be limited if users continue relying on existing tools or workflows. |

| Opportunities | Threats |
| --- | --- |
| Improve event discovery, registration, attendance tracking, and continuity between club leaders. | A privacy or security incident could reduce trust and prevent adoption. |
| Use pilot feedback and participation summaries to improve programming and outreach. | Unclear ownership or limited organizer capacity could delay decisions and ongoing maintenance. |
| Establish reusable workflows that could support future club growth if separately approved. | Scope expansion, integration needs, or institutional approvals could exceed available time and resources. |
| Improve accessibility and consistency compared with ad hoc communication and spreadsheets. | Inaccurate or outdated member records and low member engagement could undermine reporting. |

### Initial response to SWOT findings

- Confirm ownership and decision rights before committing to a delivery schedule.
- Keep the MVP narrow and validate priorities with both organizers and members.
- Agree on data minimization, retention, and access rules before using real member data.
- Pilot with a small group, measure workflow completion and feedback, then prioritize any expansion.
